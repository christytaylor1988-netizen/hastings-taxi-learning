Your licence plate permits four passengers. A group of three adults and two children asks to travel together. What should you do?

Explain that a vehicle licensed for at least five passengers is needed

Children count as passengers. The plate’s passenger limit cannot be increased by sharing a seat or paying more. Child-restraint exceptions do not increase licensed capacity.

You are driving a private hire car forwards to a pickup with no passengers. You have no other seatbelt exemption. What applies?

Wear your seatbelt

The passenger-carrying exemption does not apply on this empty trip. Wearing a belt is the default unless a specific exemption applies.

Which working situation is specifically listed as a taxi-driver seatbelt exemption?

A licensed taxi driver plying for hire at a taxi rank

GOV.UK also lists licensed taxi or private hire drivers carrying passengers. These exemptions are situation-specific, not a blanket exemption for an entire shift. Wearing a belt remains a safety choice where exempt.

GOV.UK When you do not need to wear a seat belt, taxi/private hire bullets · National legal exemption · Inspired by mock Q66

A four-year-old is travelling in a licensed taxi without the correct child car seat provided. Under the taxi exception, where and how may they travel?

On a rear seat using an adult seatbelt

The taxi exception permits children aged three or over to use an adult belt in the rear when the correct child seat is not provided. It does not increase the vehicle’s passenger capacity or establish a general front-seat minimum age.

For an ordinary taxi car, what minimum tread depth is required across the central three-quarters and around the entire tyre?

1.6 mm

The car rule is 1.6 mm over that area. Other vehicle classes can have different requirements.
