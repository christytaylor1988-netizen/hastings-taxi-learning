Expansion 02 — address checks

Checked 19 October 2026. 50 playable locations: 25 original provisional records retained, plus 25 newly address-checked records. All questions use the unchanged 305-record locations.js. All 25 additions exactly match the 305-row Brainscape CSV. No claim of council exam approval is made.

Sources below support a single road for each addition. Original wording is retained, including older venue names and road abbreviations. Answer options are deduplicated by road identity and prefer the same area or type of venue.

Still requiring checking

The original 25 remain provisional. Hastings Museum & Art Gallery was held out because John’s Place and Bohemia Road need examiner clarification. Co-op Ore and Bannatyne Spa Hotel were held out pending stronger current address evidence. Multi-road records, former schools and unclear venue names were not added. General Havelock and Shipwreck Heritage Centre retain historical database names; their roads are supported by the linked sources, but current exam naming should be confirmed.

Progress and backup

The existing progress key and all starter record IDs are retained. Existing valid progress is also copied once to hastingsLearnPrototype.beforeExpansion02 before any new saves. Invalid data is never overwritten. An unfinished lesson must be finished before switching modes. Mastered means three consecutive correct answers; needing revision means a previous mistake and fewer than three consecutive correct answers. Progress belongs to the browser and web address: the public site and local preview have separate storage.
