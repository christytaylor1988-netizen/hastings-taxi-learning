Who is responsible for submitting a complete licence renewal on time?

The applicant

Council reminders do not transfer the applicant’s responsibility.

Appendix A, 1.3; printed p31 (PDF page 32) · Application requirement

Your DBS certificate arrives at home. What does Appendix A require next?

Provide it to the council

The council says it is not sent a separate copy.

Appendix A, 2.8; printed p33 (PDF page 34) · Application requirement

What must a licensed driver maintain for DBS status checks?

An Update Service subscription and ongoing council checking permission

Appendix A requires ongoing subscription and permission to check.

Appendix A, 2.9; printed p33 (PDF page 34) · Application requirement

What is required if a DBS status check shows the certificate is no longer current?

Apply for a new Enhanced DBS certificate

Follow the new Enhanced DBS certificate procedure referenced in Appendix A.

Appendix A, 2.11; printed p33 (PDF page 34) · Application requirement

Whom may a proprietor allow to drive the licensed vehicle under Appendix C?

A driver holding the appropriate Hastings-issued taxi/private hire licence

The proprietor must check the appropriate Hastings driver licence. This is a proprietor duty, not a general statement about all cross-border bookings.

Appendix C, 23.1; printed p45 (PDF page 46) · Licence condition

Where must notices about an installed vehicle CCTV system be visible?

Both outside and inside the vehicle

Passengers must be informed by prominent notices inside and outside.

Appendix C, 27.8; printed p46 (PDF page 47) · Licence condition

Under the CCTV conditions, may audio recording be continuously active by default?

No; audio must not be continuously active by default

The policy calls for overt, targeted audio recording and recognises passenger privacy.

Appendix C, 27.7; printed p46 (PDF page 47) · Licence condition

May a driver call out to pressure people into hiring the vehicle?

No, and they must not use someone else to do it

The condition prohibits soliciting in this way, including through another person.

Appendix D, 3.5; printed p47 (PDF page 48) · Licence condition

May you bring your own pet while the licensed vehicle is plying for hire or carrying a hiring?

No

The restriction is about the driver’s animal. Passenger animals and assistance dogs have different provisions.

Appendix D, 3.10; printed p48 (PDF page 49) · Licence condition

You hold an assistance-dog medical exemption. What must be displayed while working?

The exemption certificate in the vehicle

This is an assistance-dog exemption, not an exemption from all disability duties.

Appendix D, 5.3; printed p48 (PDF page 49) · Licence condition

Who must be told about your assistance-dog exemption certificate?

Vehicle proprietors and operators

Appendix D requires notification to the proprietor and operator.

Appendix D, 5.4; printed p48 (PDF page 49) · Licence condition

Before operating a wheelchair-accessible vehicle, what must the driver understand?

Correct operation of its ramps, lifts and wheelchair restraints

This is a specific equipment competence requirement.

Appendix D, 6.1, first bullet; printed p48 (PDF page 49) · Licence condition

What must happen to the meter while a wheelchair passenger is assisted into or out of the vehicle?

It must not be kept running during that assistance

No extra charge is permitted for carrying wheelchair passengers, and the meter must not run during this assistance.

Appendix D, 6.3; printed p49 (PDF page 50) · Licence condition

If requested, what luggage help is required of a driver with a vehicle constructed to carry luggage?

Reasonable loading help and help to or from the entrance

The duty is reasonable assistance, including at the entrance of the pickup or set-down place.

Appendix D, 7.1, second and third bullets; printed p49 (PDF page 50) · Licence condition

When must a private hire vehicle be checked for property left behind?

Immediately after the hiring, or as soon as practicable afterwards

This is the private hire wording. Appendix K byelaw 16 separately addresses hackney carriages.

Appendix D, 8.1; printed p49 (PDF page 50) · Licence condition

A hirer requests a receipt. What must you provide?

A written receipt for the fare paid

The condition requires a written receipt; it says the receipt should show the journey date, badge number and amount paid.

Appendix D, 9.2; printed p49 (PDF page 50) · Licence condition

Who may inspect a private hire driver’s licence on request?

An authorised council officer or a police officer

The licence must be available for inspection by the named officers.

Appendix D, 12.5; printed p51 (PDF page 52) · Licence condition

A passenger wants to report a complaint. What identifying information must you give?

Badge number and vehicle plate and/or registration details

Give sufficient information to identify both driver and vehicle.

Appendix D, 12.6; printed p51 (PDF page 52) · Licence condition

Where must customer information about making a council complaint be shown?

Clearly within the vehicle

The driver is responsible for clear display inside the vehicle.

Appendix D, 12.7; printed p51 (PDF page 52) · Licence condition

When must the PRIVATE HIRE OPERATOR make the required booking record?

Before the journey starts

This is an operator obligation, included for working context rather than assumed driver-test content.

Appendix E, 2.2; printed p52 (PDF page 53) · Licence condition

How long must the operator keep the booking records under Appendix E?

At least six months after the entry

Do not confuse booking retention with other records, which have different periods.

Appendix E, 2.3; printed p52 (PDF page 53) · Licence condition

How does Appendix F say each suitability application is considered?

On its own merits

This is the council’s decision-making policy, not a guarantee of any licensing outcome.

Appendix F, 1.3; printed p54 (PDF page 55) · Council decision policy

Are offences treated as less serious simply because the person was off duty?

No

The policy says being off duty does not itself make an offence less serious.

Appendix F, 1.10; printed p55 (PDF page 56) · Council decision policy

Which consideration is explicitly relevant to an enforcement decision?

Danger to the public

Other listed considerations include seriousness, history and effectiveness of the action.

Appendix G, 2.2; printed p59 (PDF page 60) · Council enforcement policy

What happens to an immediate public-safety suspension while an appeal is heard?

It remains in force

This question concerns an immediate driver suspension in the interest of public safety, not every suspension.

Appendix G, 9.5.1; printed p63 (PDF page 64) · Council enforcement policy

What is the maximum number of council penalty points issued on one occasion under Appendix J?

12

Several breaches can arise from one incident, but the stated maximum on one occasion is 12. These are council points, not DVLA points.

Appendix J, 4; printed p67 (PDF page 68) · Council penalty scheme

How must the issue of council penalty points be confirmed?

In writing to the licence holder

Appendix J requires written confirmation.

Appendix J, 5; printed p67 (PDF page 68) · Council penalty scheme

How long does Appendix J allow for a written appeal against council penalty points?

14 days from the date of the notice

Write to the Licensing Team and state the reasons why the points should not be awarded. This is not the court-appeal deadline for a revoked licence.

Appendix J, 6; printed p67 (PDF page 68) · Council penalty scheme

How long do these council penalty points remain live?

12 months from imposition

The scheme considers points in a rolling 12-month period.

Appendix J, 7; printed p67 (PDF page 68) · Council penalty scheme

What can accumulating 12 council points in 12 months lead to?

A possible Sub Committee hearing about the licence

The appendix says a hearing may be required. Outcomes can include a warning, suspension or revocation; none is automatic here.

Appendix J, 8; printed p67 (PDF page 68) · Council penalty scheme

Does the council points scheme prevent other enforcement action?

No

The scheme operates without removing other action available under legislation or policy.

Appendix J, 10; printed p67 (PDF page 68) · Council penalty scheme

According to byelaw 9(b), what should a hackney driver do if a taxi stand is full?

Proceed to another stand

The byelaw does not authorise overflowing a full stand.

Which clothing option is accepted by the driver dress code?

Smart jeans

The dress code lists smart jeans among acceptable options.

Appendix L, 2, trousers bullet; printed p76 (PDF page 77) · Dress-code condition (D 1.3)

How long should tailored shorts be under the dress code?

Reach the knee when standing upright

This is the stated standard for tailored shorts.

Appendix L, 2, trousers bullet; printed p76 (PDF page 77) · Dress-code condition (D 1.3)

Which footwear meets the dress-code requirement?

Footwear secured around the heel and safe for driving

Heel security and safe vehicle operation matter.

Appendix L, 2 footwear bullet; 3 footwear bullet; printed p76 (PDF page 77) · Dress-code condition (D 1.3)

Which clothing is listed as unacceptable?

Clothing with suggestive or potentially offensive graphics

The dress code restricts suggestive or potentially offensive wording, logos or graphics.

Appendix L, 3, words/logos/graphics bullet; printed p76 (PDF page 77) · Dress-code condition (D 1.3)

Does the disability carriage-and-assistance duty concern only wheelchair users?

No, it extends to disabled passengers generally

The extension covers disabled passengers generally. Actual duties depend on circumstances, vehicle suitability and valid exemptions.

Appendix M, 2; printed p76 (PDF page 77) · Statutory duty summary

Before a pre-booked journey you are told a disabled passenger needs help finding your vehicle. What is required?

Help them identify and find the vehicle

Cross-checked with DfT guidance 1.5.3 and 13: advance notice that assistance is needed triggers the duty; no extra charge.

Appendix M, 3; printed p76 (PDF page 77) · Statutory duty summary

A suitable vehicle is available. May a PHV operator refuse a booking solely because the passenger is disabled?

No

The prohibition covers discriminatory refusal. This question expressly assumes a suitable vehicle is available.

Appendix M, 6; printed p77 (PDF page 78) · Statutory duty summary

Which licensing authority can suspend or revoke a driver’s licence?

The authority that issued that driver’s licence

This is a licensing-authority power, not a deadline or duty imposed on the driver.

Appendix N, “Reporting safeguarding or road safety concerns”, paragraph 2; printed p77 (PDF page 78) · Licensing-authority statutory duty

Once the statutory reporting conditions are met, how long does an English licensing authority have to report concerns to the issuing authority?

10 working days

This is the council-to-council safeguarding/road-safety reporting period; not the driver’s separate notification rules.

Appendix N, “Reporting safeguarding or road safety concerns”, paragraph beginning “Once”; printed p78 (PDF page 79) · Licensing-authority statutory duty

Which conduct is explicitly listed as a safeguarding or road-safety concern?

Harassing another person

The list also covers other serious harmful conduct and road-safety risk.

Appendix N, “What counts as safeguarding and road safety concerns?”, harassment bullet; printed p78 (PDF page 79) · Licensing-authority statutory duty

After receiving a relevant report, how long does the issuing authority have to tell the reporting authority its decision or intended action?

20 working days

The response must be written and give reasons. This is an authority duty, not a driver response deadline.
